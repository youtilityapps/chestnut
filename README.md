Let Chestnut stay on top of your agents, so you can stay focused on what really needs your attention.

## What it does

- **Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode in one window.** Every session runs in its own tab, and sessions you started elsewhere show up too.
- **Every session at a glance.** Each one gets a card with its title, progress and status. Sessions for the same ticket group together, and helpers fold under the session that started them.
- **Everything a session touches, right beside it.** The Inspector shows its timeline, its pull requests and their checks, and the files and links it produced.
- **Every conversation, one search away.** Search across all your sessions, messages and pull requests. Each conversation gets an automatic summary and timeline.
- **See where work went wrong.** Chestnut points out the friction in a session, so you can fix the setup instead of the prompt.
- **You stay in charge.** When an agent asks to start a session or change a project, Chestnut holds the request until you say yes.

## Your work stays yours

Your conversations live on your Mac, and Chestnut uploads none of them. You don't need an account to use it. Crash reports are off until you turn them on.

## Install

Chestnut runs on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 13 Ventura or later.

1. [Download `chestnut-arm64.dmg`](https://github.com/youtilityapps/chestnut/releases/latest/download/chestnut-arm64.dmg).
2. Open it and drag Chestnut into your Applications folder.

Chestnut is signed and notarized by Apple, and it tells you when a new version is ready to install.
